Welcome to Vidnets Studio

Create a polished video in a few clear steps

This guide covers the everyday workflow: connect your footage sources, prepare a script, choose a look, and start a render. You do not need to understand the technical details behind the scenes.

1

First-time setup

Connect your accounts

Open Settings. Add your OpenRouter key for scene intelligence. Pexels and Pixabay keys are optional, but they give the app more stock-footage choices.

Keep keys private. Vidnets stores them in the desktop app settings and never asks you to paste them into a script.

2

YouTube footage

Connect cookies once

Click Connect cookies, choose Chrome or Edge, sign in to YouTube in the dedicated window, and leave the window open until Vidnets confirms the account.

  • Use the account that should download footage.
  • Do not close the sign-in window early.
  • Reconnect if YouTube reports an expired session.
3

Writing the story

Prepare a strong script

Paste the narration you want spoken. Write in short paragraphs so each idea can receive its own visual.

  • Use full names, places, games, and dates.
  • Keep one main topic per video.
  • Separate confirmed facts from speculation.
  • Aim for roughly 130–160 spoken words per minute.
4

Choose the look

Use simple starting settings

  • Mixed Media: the best default for documentaries.
  • YouTube Clips: choose this when motion footage is the priority.
  • Web Images: choose this for a still-image story.
  • Captions: keep them on for short-form and social videos.
  • Use 16:9 for YouTube, or 9:16 for Shorts and Reels.
5

Render workflow

From script to finished video

Quick help

When something is not right

  • No YouTube clips: reconnect cookies from the banner or Settings.
  • Missing stock: add a Pexels or Pixabay key.
  • Want another attempt: use Queue → Resume.
  • Need older exports: open Projects and select any thumbnail.
